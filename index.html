Comodelling


Comodelling is a R&D initiative to facilitate modelling efforts towards collective knowledge and agency.

We are looking for new ways to co-produce and co-model information: for, by, and across communities.
What types of information do you think we should collect, model, share, visualise?
What tools, platforms, and processes should we use or develop to do that?

Our main areas of interest currently are:
  • Coordinated actions towards social change. What are we doing? What should we do?
  • Collective assessments of information. What's reliable, worthy of our collective effort?
  • Collaboration mechanisms towards consensus-building, accountability, and bias minimisation. Whom are our processes serving?

Our projects are free, open source, and community-driven.

Comodelling is an open, emerging initiative.
Contact us to join the effort, collaborate, send us any thought or feedback.
