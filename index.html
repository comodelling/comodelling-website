Comodelling


Comodelling aims to equip communities with tools and information systems leveraging their collective intelligence and agency, for their benefit and that of the wider ecosystem.

While we plan to crowdsource the questions of what to comodel and how, here are some of the things we're interested in:

  • interconnecting common objectives
  • mapping potential actions and outcomes
  • evaluating the factuality of claims
  • monitoring common resources

Our first project, CommonGraph, is a tool to design platforms for co-producing, connecting and rating information with configurable graph and collaboration options.

Comodelling is an emerging, non-profit initiative.
For any question or interest, feel free to contact us.
