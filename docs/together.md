# Together

We are envisioning different futures for online collaboration, powered by human, collective agency.

Everyone's vision and skills matter when it comes to co-modelling our future.<br>

We would love to hear from you.<br>
[Get in touch](mailto:contact@comodelling.org) if you have any feedback, questions, ideas, or would like to join the initiative.

You can also contribute in various ways to our current efforts:

- beta-testing [our platforms](./projects.md#commonoutcomes)
- improving [our software](https://github.com/comodelling/comodelling-website/)
- developing [our wiki](./wiki/index.md)
