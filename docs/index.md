<h1>Comodelling</h1>

Comodelling is a R&D initiative to facilitate efforts towards collective agency and knowledge.

<!-- At its core, a conviction: ***we** can do better*. -->

We are looking for new ways to [co-model](wiki/co-modelling/index.md) information: community-driven, intentional, structured.

We invite everyone to join us in asking: [*what are the things we should model together*?](./wiki/co-modelling/index.md#what)

Our current research directions can also be framed as questions to be co-answered:

* Informing and coordinating  action towards social change: what should we be doing?
* Collective assessment of information: what's reliable, consensual, representative...?
* Mechanisms and processes for effective collaboration: what are we actually achieving and whom is it benefiting?

Explore our [wiki](wiki/index.md) for more info, or jump straight into our [projects](projects.md).

Comodelling is an open, emerging initiative. [Get in touch](together.md).
