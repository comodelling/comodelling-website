# Comodelling Wiki

This wiki provides some context, grounding and directions for our [projects](../projects.md).<br>
It is evolving, open to suggestions, both in terms of content and management.
You are welcome to ask questions or suggest improvements via [issues](https://github.com/comodelling/comodelling-website/issues) or [PRs](https://github.com/comodelling/comodelling-website/pulls), or [email](mailto:contact@comodelling.org).


We'd love it if this wiki could be useful to others too.
