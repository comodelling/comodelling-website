# Polls

Polls offer a way to gather structured, subjective inputs from people regarding specific topics.
They generally take the form of a question addressed to a [community](communities.md), where each and everyone answers following their own judgement.
Possible answers are generally limited among a finite set number of options or a range of values.

Polls can be used for different purposes in the context of co-modelling: either to help assessing some target measures, or integrated into the collaborative editing process.
