# Collective practices

What practices should co-modellers engage in?

Thinking about different practices offer a widening angle to the co-modelling process, going beyond the mere definition and application of a shared model.
Different practices might help us think out of the box, helping us to think adaptively and reflectively with respect to our objectives.

Here is a non-exhaustive list of practices related to co-modelling:

* ***envisioning***, imagining, possibilities at different levels,
* ***defining*** the terms, the models and parameters that foster co-creation,
* ***inputing***, voicing, what's relevant in different contexts,
* ***relating***, linking, different elements and problems together,
* ***questioning***, interrogating, as a constructive community enquiry,
* ***assessing***, rating, evaluating, some measures,

We could imagine activities both digital or in person to facilitate those practices.
